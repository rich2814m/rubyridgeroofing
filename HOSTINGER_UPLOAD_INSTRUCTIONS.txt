This folder is the complete website.

HOSTINGER:
1. Log in to Hostinger hPanel.
2. Open Websites > Manage > File Manager.
3. Open the public_html folder for your domain.
4. Upload the CONTENTS of this folder into public_html.
5. Make sure index.html is directly inside public_html (not inside another nested folder).
6. Visit your domain in Safari/Chrome.
